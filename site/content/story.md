This is the kitchen, the living room and the upstairs floors of a house in Edmonds, drawn from the contractor's cabinet plan and five photographs. It is for a family of four: two adults who cook, and two children of 5 and 8 who will grow up in it.

The brief asked for a rich bright lower cabinet and a lighter upper, both high gloss, both with an integrated handle. No store sells that. The design uses IKEA boxes with lacquer fronts that an online maker sprays in any RAL colour. The integrated handle on those fronts is seen in one image only, and the owner must confirm it before an order.

The refrigerator moves to the hall end of the long wall, as the contractor drew it. That move puts the refrigerator far from the only sink, and the first check of the cook's walk failed. A small prep sink in the island corrects it. The old refrigerator alcove becomes an open pantry, with the microwave on a low shelf that the smaller child can reach.

The job is checked against the budget with sales tax and a contingency. To stay inside it, the owner assembles the cabinet boxes and the floor is a plank from a local store. The prices are web prices and store tags. None is a quote.
