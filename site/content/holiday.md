On a holiday one person stands at the range while another carries a dish past. The aisle between the range and the island is at the width for that. Each side of the island has more than the minimum passage.

People also walk behind the island seats. That space depends on where the dining table stands, and the table is not in the model. Measure it with tape on the floor.
